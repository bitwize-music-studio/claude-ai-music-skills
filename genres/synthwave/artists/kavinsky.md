# Kavinsky — Deep Dive

## Overview

Kavinsky was the French electronic musician Vincent Belorgey (1975-2026), one of the first artists of what became synthwave. Working in Paris from 2005, he built his records around a character, a man killed in his Ferrari Testarossa in 1986 who returns as a zombie, and around the sound of 1980s action films, cop shows and arcade games: brooding minor-key synths, pounding electro beats, wailing rock guitar and processed "zombie" vocals. "Nightcall" (2010), co-written and produced with Daft Punk's Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo, opened the film *Drive* in 2011 and became his signature. He released two albums, *OutRun* (2013) and *Reborn* (2022), performed "Nightcall" at the Paris 2024 Olympics closing ceremony, and died at his Paris home on 28 July 2026, aged 50.

**Genre positioning:** Synthwave, outrun, French electro house. Wikipedia's synthwave article names David Grellier (College) and Kavinsky among "the earliest artists to be part of the emergence of synthwave"; early press called it "retro electro". RA (2026) called him one of the movement's signature artists. He came out of the Paris electro scene of the 2000s rather than an online synthwave community: he opened for Daft Punk on their 2007 tour, and Ed Banger's SebastiAn co-wrote and mixed *OutRun*.

**Commercial scale:** "Nightcall" is certified Diamond in France, Platinum in the US and Gold in the UK; the 2024 re-recording with Angèle and Phoenix reached No. 3 in France, was certified Diamond, and became the most-Shazamed song ever in a single day after the Olympics ceremony. *OutRun* reached No. 2 in France and won the 2014 Victoires de la musique for electronic album.

**Key quote:** "My idea was to create an excuse for a story to make music for." — Kavinsky, 2013

## The Kavinsky Character

His label's version: "Kavinsky died in a car crash in 1986 in Los Angeles, whilst driving his beloved Testarossa." He "re-appeared as a zombie twenty years later" making electronic music. On covers he has red eyes and blue skin. Belorgey really did drive a Testarossa.

- "Kavinsky is a character I created because just putting my face on a record sleeve isn't something that suits me." (2007)
- "It's a character, it's not really me. My name is Vincent." (2013)
- "I decided to create a guy in a car who is dead, so he can die again, and then just have him drive around." (2013)

*OutRun* tells the story as an album, with spoken prologue and epilogue by Paul Hahn (Daft Punk's manager); its title nods to the Sega arcade game: "a tribute, you could say, to a game that I used to play a lot."

## Sonic Evolution Summary

| Era | Releases | Sound |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 2005-2008 | *Teddy Boy*, *1986*, *Blazer* EPs | Raw retro electro: drum machines, synth riffs, short instrumental "scenes" from the character's story |
| 2010-2013 | "Nightcall", *OutRun* | Made entirely in software, "fast and furious… full throttle/full compression": Moroder-style synth sequences, house beats, hair-metal guitar solos, vocoded vocals |
| 2022 | *Reborn* | "Mostly all real instruments": 808s and Roland Jupiters, saxophone, strings, guitar; electro-funk and duets; "more lush, more soft, less tortured" |

**Tempo range:** Spotify-derived readings for the main tracks run from about 73 ("Suburbia", a hip-hop track) to 130 BPM ("Testarossa Autodrive"), mostly 105-125. Most are in minor keys.

- **Release date**: February 2013 (22-26 February by territory)
- **Label**: Record Makers; Mercury (Germany), Casablanca/Republic (US)
- **Producers**: Kavinsky, SebastiAn, Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo
- **Recording**: 2006-2012, Gang, CBE and Third Side studios, Paris; mixed by SebastiAn

- Rock guitar solos (credited to "Raw Man" on "Blizzard", "ProtoVision" and "First Blood")
- Processed, vocoded male vocal; guest rappers and singers
- Spoken prologue and epilogue framing a story

### Reborn (2022)

- **Release date**: 25 March 2022
- **Label**: Record Makers (REC190); Virgin France; Astralwerks (US)
- **Producers**: Kavinsky, Gaspard Augé, Victor Le Masne
- **Recording**: Motorbass, Paris; mixed by Michael Declerck
- **Length**: 47:17
- **Chart**: UK Dance 3, France 13
- **Reception**: Metacritic 76

His second and last album. Kavinsky: "it's mostly all real instruments on the record. Everything opened up when I went back into the studio with all these new machines." The label: "its 808s and Jupiters proudly make their way into the sonic spectrum", with "hints of 80's Japanese anime music". He told NME: "If you compare the last album to driving a fast car, this is more about driving the car slowly." **Sonic Palette:**
- Roland TR-808 drums and Jupiter synths, played rather than programmed in plug-ins
- Saxophone (Adrien Soleiman on "Zenith"), strings, guitars
- Electro-funk grooves; softer and more lush than *OutRun*
- Male-female duets and guest singers on most tracks

## "Nightcall"

- **Credits**: Written by Kavinsky and Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo; produced by Guy-Man; mixed by SebastiAn
- **Vocals**: Kavinsky sings the verses through heavy processing, the label's "spooky, distorted, zombie vocals", "heavily vocoded" according to a third-party analysis. Lovefoxxx sings the chorus in a smooth, clear voice, the label's "sultry purr"
- **Harmony** (third-party transcription): verse Am–G–F–Dm, chorus F–G–Em–F; A minor, ~91 BPM
- ***Drive***: Opens the film's soundtrack and its opening credits. Kavinsky: "When I heard my song in the movie, I screamed in the cinema", and "It was the best visualization we could have for 'Nightcall.'" By 2022: "I want people to talk to me about something other than 'Nightcall' now"
- **2024**: Performed with Angèle (lead vocal) and Phoenix at the Olympics closing ceremony in the Stade de France; re-recorded with them
- **Certifications**: France Diamond, US Platinum, UK Gold, Germany Gold, New Zealand Platinum

## Musical Analysis

### Images First

"When I'm making music, I have images in my head of films and television shows I liked when I was little." His sources were eighties childhood culture: "The Pong, all the Nintendos, all the Segas. The Goonies, G.I. Joe, the Spielberg movies. I'm truly a sponge", and film themes: "The Terminator theme — it's very simple but does a lot to you." The tracks work as scenes in a story: the crash, the night drive, the return.

### The Software Era

Up to *OutRun* he worked only in the computer ("Logic is just a big trick"), with no analog keyboards and no formal training. The sound is dense and loud: synth bass sequences and arpeggios, house and electro drums, rock guitar solos, and heavy compression throughout.

### Vocals

His own voice is processed into a robotic or "zombie" tone, set against clear female or guest vocals: the Kavinsky-and-Lovefoxxx contrast on "Nightcall", repeated on "Zenith". Rappers (Havoc, Tyson) appear on *OutRun*; *Reborn* uses guest singers on most tracks.

### The Real-Instrument Turn

*Reborn* reverses the method: real drum machines (808) and synths (Roland Jupiters), saxophone, strings and guitars, and softer, funkier grooves. "I hate happy music," he had said in 2013 (Hunger TV, quoted by Crack); *Reborn* is still minor-key and nocturnal, but warmer.

### Genre Classification

- **Synthwave / outrun**: The primary label; among its first artists
- **French electro house**: His scene and collaborators (Daft Punk, SebastiAn, Justice)
- **Electro-funk**: *Reborn*
