### Big Bad Voodoo Daddy

- "Go Daddy-O" (1996) — Mid-tempo swing anthem; the Super Bowl number
- "Mr. Pinstripe Suit" (1998) — Swaggering minor-key swing
- "Minnie the Moocher" (1998) — Calloway call-and-response
- "Jumpin' Jack" (1998) — Bright jump with an Ellington quote
- "Maddest Kind of Love" (1998) — Minor-key swing
- "2000 Volts" (1999) — Fast six-minute jump
- "Simple Songs" (2003) — New Orleans-flavoured swing
- "Why Me?" (2012) — Later single, recorded largely live
- "Jump, Jive An' Wail" (2017) — The Louis Prima tribute

### Cherry Poppin' Daddies

- "Sockable Face Club" (1994) — Ska-punk
- "Here Comes the Snake" (1996) — Mid-tempo minor-key swing
- "Zoot Suit Riot" (1997) — Fast minor-key jump blues with a full sax-and-trumpet section; their signature
- "Brown Derby Jump" (1997) — Very fast jump
- "Come Back to Me" (1997) — Crooner standard in 3/4
- "Diamond Light Boogie" (2000) — Glam-rock boogie

- "I Love American Music" (2013) — Return to swing
- "Diesel PunX" (2019) — Political ska-punk
