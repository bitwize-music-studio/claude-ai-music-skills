## The plugin's rules now reach your sessions

Nothing in your content, audio or documents folders changes, and there is nothing to install.

### What changed for me as a user?

- **Sessions in your music workspace follow the plugin's rules from the start.** Claude routes album names to `/bitwize-music:resume`, a new album to `/bitwize-music:new-album`, asks about every homograph before Suno sees it, blocks generation until sources are verified, and never picks a take for you. These rules were written for every session but, because of how Claude Code loads plugins, never reached plugin users before.
- **Where you start Claude Code matters.** The rules load when the session starts in or below `content_root`, `audio_root` or `documents_root`. Started elsewhere, the session gets a one-line note pointing at the rules instead, so the plugin doesn't add music rules to your other projects.
- **After `/clear` and when a long session compacts**, the rules are added again.
